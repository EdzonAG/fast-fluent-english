Destinations

Where we go in Chiapas

A jungle base by turquoise lakes — plus the classic wonders of Chiapas, if you want them in your route.

Lagos de Colón

The heart of the expedition: turquoise lakes, natural pools and waterfalls near the Guatemalan border, with native hosts and cabin lodges.

Mayan ruins

Toniná

A monumental Mayan city with one of the tallest stepped acropolises in Mesoamerica.

Lakes

Montebello

Lagunas de Montebello National Park: dozens of lakes in shades of blue and green among pine forests.

Colonial town

San Cristóbal de las Casas

Highland town of cobbled streets, colorful markets and living indigenous culture.

Canyon

Cañón del Sumidero

A boat ride between canyon walls that rise up to about 1,000 m above the river.

Colonial town

Chiapa de Corzo

Riverside colonial town and gateway to the Sumidero canyon, famous for its 16th-century fountain.

    Anything else? Send us a WhatsApp with your questions or the best day and time for a call.

    How long is an expedition?

    As long as you like: you can travel for only a weekend or for a complete month.

    How do payments work?

    A €50 reservation, then +€200 six weeks before (at our 1st Zoom meeting) and +€300 two weeks before (at our 2nd Zoom meeting).

    Do we stay in hotels?

    No hotels and no tourist crowds. You stay in comfortable rustic cabin lodges with native hosts and eat home-cooked regional food.

    Can I customize my trip?

    Yes. We offer last-minute, common-sense flexibility, and your adjustments and suggestions are always welcome.
