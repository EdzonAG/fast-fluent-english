The FAST method

You talk. We guide, explain and correct.

In a FAST class the student talks most of the time about real life and real work. Every correction goes into your own personalized e-book — a quick reference that grows with you.

Talk about your real life

Your family, your weekend, your plans, the news. Everyday topics that build natural, spontaneous English — the kind you actually use.

Business communication

Meetings, negotiations, emails, calls and small talk with international colleagues — practiced with the vocabulary of your own industry.

Job interview practice

Rehearse real interview questions, polish your answers and walk into your next interview with confidence.

Presentation skills

Organize, rehearse and deliver presentations, lectures and trainings — with feedback on structure, pronunciation and delivery.

Your personalized e-book

During class you type notes and corrections in your own file — “FAST English A1”, for example. It becomes a quick-reference book made just for you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have doubts? Send us a WhatsApp — we're happy to help.

Ask on WhatsApp
How are the classes delivered?

All classes are live on Zoom. You book your sessions one day ahead through Calendly, choosing the time that suits you best.

How do I know my level?

We offer a free evaluation of your English level according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (A1 to C2). Book a call or phone us directly.

Can I reschedule a class?

Yes. Classes can be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice.

What do I need for class?

A laptop or desktop computer with Zoom, and a file named “FAST English A1” (or your level) where you'll build your personal e-book. Cellphones are for emergencies only.

Can my company pay for my classes?

Absolutely. We issue official commercial invoices, can bill your employer directly and provide certificates of completion for HR.

What schedules are available?

Mornings, afternoons, evenings and weekends. Choose 2 or 3 classes a week.
